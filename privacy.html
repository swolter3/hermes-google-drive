Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Purpose

Hermes Google Drive is a private integration used only by its owner. It allows a locally operated Hermes Agent to perform Google Drive actions explicitly requested by the owner.

Data accessed

The integration may access files, folders, and associated metadata in the owner's Google Drive. The exact data accessed depends on the owner's request.

Use and sharing

Google Drive data is used only to carry out the owner's requested actions. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally in the owner's Hermes installation and are not stored in this public repository. Temporary copies created during requested operations are removed when no longer needed.

Retention and deletion

Any downloaded content is retained only when the owner asks to keep it. The owner can revoke access at any time in Google Account security settings and can delete locally stored credentials.

Contact

Questions can be submitted through the repository issue tracker.
